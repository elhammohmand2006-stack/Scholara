GPA rubric

The simple 4.0 scale used by this project

A · 90 ≤ grade ≤ 1004.0
B · 80 ≤ grade < 903.0
C · 70 ≤ grade < 802.0
D · 60 ≤ grade < 701.0
F · 0 ≤ grade < 600.0

GPA = Σ (grade points × credits) ÷ total credits.

Average grade is the unweighted mean of course percentages. Your institution may use a different grading scale.
